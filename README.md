### Two local businesses, one owner

**[Deals Outlet](https://dealsoutlet.net)** — bin stores in Byron, Warner Robins and Gainesville, GA, and Tallahassee, FL. Every item in the bin starts at $10 on restock day and drops $2 a day down to $1. Nightly online auctions run in each store's Facebook group.

**[PhoneHub](https://www.phonehub.net)** — repairs for phones, tablets, watches, computers and game consoles at 10 stores across Georgia. Every repair comes with a 30-day warranty, and most are done in one to two hours.
